About Me
My name is Shane Weimar. I am a mathematics Ph.D. student at Auburn University. I am interested primarily in pure mathematics that helps us understand God and the universe he made. Specifically, I am interested in mathematics that helps us understand the strangeness of quantum mechanics (e.g. representation theory, or applications of algebraic geometry to quantum spin). Of particular interest is the idea that physics isn't necessarily about things that we think are "real", like the integers, or multiplication, but that it is sometimes instead about relations between equivilance classes.
